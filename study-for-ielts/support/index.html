Reset Learning Data

Open Study for IELTS, select Privacy & Support, then choose Reset learning data. This removes local progress, review history and flashcard ratings and disables scheduled reminders.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Speaking practice requires microphone and speech-recognition permission. You can review or change these permissions in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security.
