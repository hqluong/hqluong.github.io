Study for IELTS Privacy Policy and Support

Developer: Hung Luong

Important Independent-App and Trademark Notice

Study for IELTS is independently developed. It is not affiliated with, authorized, approved, sponsored or endorsed by the British Council, IDP IELTS, Cambridge University Press & Assessment or any IELTS Partner. IELTS is a registered trademark of the IELTS Partners.

This app provides original practice and automated study estimates only. It does not administer the IELTS test, reproduce official test questions, provide official scores, or promise any band score, improvement, admission, immigration outcome or other result.

Information We Collect

The developer does not collect, sell or use personal data for advertising or analytics. The app has no accounts, advertising SDKs or tracking technologies.

Data Stored on Your iPhone

Study progress, answers, estimated bands, lesson completion, flashcard ratings, review schedules, preferences and local reminder settings are stored on your iPhone using Apple system storage.

Speaking transcripts are processed to generate rubric-based feedback. Some transcript text may be stored locally in review history and remains on your iPhone until you reset learning data or delete the app. The app does not save raw microphone audio or send recordings or transcripts to the developer.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Microphone and speech-recognition access is requested only when you start a speaking exercise. When supported, recognition is required to run on-device. On devices where on-device recognition is unavailable, Apple's Speech service may process audio according to Apple's privacy terms.

Notifications and Widget

Optional study reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. The Home Screen widget displays vocabulary bundled with the app. The developer does not operate a remote push-notification service and receives no widget activity.

Data Sharing

The developer does not share app data with third parties.

Retention and Deletion

Local learning data remains on your iPhone until you reset it or delete the app. Use Privacy & Support > Reset learning data to remove progress, review history, flashcard ratings and the daily vocabulary study plan, and disable scheduled reminders. Deleting the app removes its remaining local data.

External Links

The app may open reference websites in your browser. Those websites process visits under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated to reflect product or legal changes. The effective date above will be revised when the policy changes.
