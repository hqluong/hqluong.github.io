Privacy Policy

Learning progress stays on your iPhone. No account, advertising or analytics is built into version 1.0.

Effective September 23, 2026Developer: Hung Luong

Important independent-app and trademark notice

Study for IELTS is independently developed. It is not affiliated with, authorized, approved, sponsored or endorsed by the British Council, IDP IELTS, Cambridge University Press & Assessment or any IELTS Partner. IELTS is a registered trademark of the IELTS Partners.

This app provides original practice and automated study estimates only. It does not administer the IELTS test, reproduce official test questions, provide official scores, or promise any band score, improvement, admission, immigration outcome or other result.

At a glance

This policy covers Study for IELTS version 1.0 and these app information pages. The app has no developer-operated backend, user accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking. The developer does not receive your learning activity, answers, recordings or transcripts through the app.

Apple may process audio when its on-device speech recognition is unavailable. If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send. These situations are explained below.

Learning data on your iPhone

The app stores study progress, answers, automated study estimates, lesson completion, flashcard ratings, review history and schedules, preferences, and reminder settings locally using Apple system storage.

Speaking transcripts are processed for text-based study feedback. Some transcript text may remain in local review history. The app does not save raw microphone audio as a recording file or upload recordings or transcripts to the developer. It does not provide account-based cloud sync.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app requests Microphone and Speech Recognition permission when you start a speaking exercise. When your device supports on-device recognition, the app requires recognition to run on-device. Otherwise, Apple's Speech service may process audio remotely under Apple's privacy terms.

You can decline or later change these permissions in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security. Speaking transcription needs the relevant permissions; the other study features remain available. Apple manages its system services under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Reminders and vocabulary widget

Optional study reminders use local iPhone notifications. The developer does not operate a remote push-notification service. You can turn reminders off in the app or change notification permission in iPhone Settings.

The Home Screen widget displays vocabulary bundled with the app. The developer receives no widget activity.

Retention and deletion

Local learning information remains on your device until you reset it or delete the app. Open Today → Privacy & Support → Reset learning data and confirm to remove progress, review history, flashcard ratings and the daily vocabulary study plan, and disable scheduled reminders.

Deleting the app removes its remaining local data. Offloading an app can preserve its documents and data. Any device backups are controlled separately through Apple and are not deleted by resetting the app. The developer cannot access or recover your local learning data.

When you contact support

If you email the developer, your email address, message and any attachments are received to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. This correspondence is handled through email services and is retained only as needed to resolve support requests and meet applicable obligations. It is not used for advertising.

You can use the contact below to ask about or request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information, private recordings or passwords.

This website and external links

These pages have no developer-added analytics, tracking scripts, embedded advertising or contact forms. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement.

The app and these pages may link to reference websites or Apple services. When you visit those sites or send an email, the relevant service's own privacy terms apply.

Data sharing and future changes

The developer does not sell your personal information or share your app learning data with advertisers. Version 1.0 has no advertising or analytics collection. If a future version changes these practices, this policy and the relevant app disclosures will be updated to explain the change and any available choices.
