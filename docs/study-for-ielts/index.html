Four skills, one routine

Vocabulary in context

Use flashcards, natural examples and Vietnamese meanings. Keep a small vocabulary reminder on your Home Screen with the widget.

Review that follows your work

Revisit mistakes, use your personal error notebook and work through scheduled recall. Optional local reminders help you return.

Practice support, with clear limits.

Automated feedback and band estimates are study aids. They are not validated predictions of a test result. Speech transcription turns spoken practice into text; it does not establish pronunciation accuracy or an official Speaking score.

Learning progress is stored on your iPhone. Speaking practice may use Apple's Speech service when on-device recognition is unavailable. See the Privacy Policy for details.
