Bonjour Path · Versions 1.0–1.2

Privacy Policy

Your learning progress stays on your iPhone. Optional usage sharing and advertising have separate privacy controls.

Updated October 23, 2026 Operator: Hung Luong

About versions: Version 1.0 does not include Firebase Analytics. The optional analytics described below applies to versions 1.1 and later, beginning with the Share app usage control, and starts only if you agree.

Your choices

Bonjour Path does not require an account. Versions 1.0 and 1.1 have no advertising. Version 1.2 introduces Google AdMob advertisements to help support the app. Learning progress is stored on your device. In versions with the Share app usage control, optional usage analytics helps the developer understand which learning features are useful. Sharing is off until you agree. Every learning feature works with sharing turned off.

You can change your choice at Progress → Privacy. Closing the initial choice without agreeing leaves sharing off.

Learning information stored on your device

The app stores course progress, answers and attempts, review schedules, checkpoints, writing drafts, saved phrases, app preferences, reminder settings, and widget preferences locally. Recordings, transcripts, written answers, saved phrases, backup files and backup passwords are never included in the app's analytics events.

Optional Google Firebase Analytics

If you agree to share app usage, Bonjour Path uses Google Analytics for Firebase to send:

  • Visits to Today, Path, Practice, Words and Progress.
  • Course lesson completion, with the learning level.
  • Completed course practice checks, with the learning level and skill category. Answers, scores and correctness are not sent in these custom events.
  • Standard analytics information such as app launches, sessions, engagement, app version, operating system, device model, language, an app-instance identifier and approximate location derived from network information.

These reports help the developer understand feature use and improve the app. An app-instance identifier distinguishes one installation from another; this is pseudonymous information, not a promise of anonymity. The app does not set an account/user ID in Firebase Analytics, and its Firebase configuration disables advertising and vendor identifier collection and advertising personalization. These optional reports are not used for advertising. Advertising SDK processing in version 1.2 is described separately below. The vocabulary widget includes neither Firebase Analytics nor AdMob.

Google processes analytics as the service provider and may process information outside your country. See Google's Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Turning sharing off stops future analytics collection and clears analytics stored locally, including resetting its app-instance identifier. It does not remove reports already sent to Google or change your learning progress. Previously sent information is subject to the Google Analytics property's retention settings. Use the privacy contact below for questions or deletion requests.

Google AdMob advertising in version 1.2

Version 1.2 includes banner ads on Today and Progress and occasional full-screen ads when you return to those screens after about 90 seconds of active use. Interstitials are spaced at least 30 minutes apart and limited to two per calendar day. The app does not run an advertising timer that interrupts an exercise.

Google Mobile Ads and its User Messaging Platform may process your IP address and approximate location, app- or device-related identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app/device information, crash logs and performance or other diagnostic information. This information supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data collection; it does not mean that no information is processed.

This version requests non-personalized ads, disables the Google publisher first-party identifier and does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. The app does not send your recordings, transcripts, lesson answers, writing drafts or saved phrases to AdMob.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether a privacy message is required before the advertising SDK starts or requests ads. Where required, you can review or change the available choices at Progress → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. If consent information cannot be updated, the app does not start a new ad request. Learning remains available if an ad cannot be shown.

Advertising is separate from Share app usage. Turning off that switch stops optional Firebase Analytics; it does not remove advertisements or change your advertising choices. Google may process advertising information outside your country and retain it under its service policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses advertising information.

Microphone, recordings and speech recognition

Microphone access is optional and requested only when you choose to record a speaking-practice response. Recordings are temporary local files for replay or transcription. Old temporary recordings are removed. The app does not upload recordings to the developer or Firebase.

Optional speech transcription requires Apple's on-device Canadian French recognition. If it is unavailable, the app does not send your recording for remote transcription. The transcript is practice assistance, not an accent score or official assessment. Apple manages system permissions and speech technology under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Reminders and backups

Learning reminders are local iOS notifications. You control notification permission in iPhone Settings.

You may export a password-protected encrypted learning backup to a destination you choose. Encryption and restoration happen on your device. The developer does not receive the backup or password and cannot recover a forgotten password. The destination you select has its own privacy terms. Analytics consent, advertising consent and local advertising frequency counters are stored separately and are not restored from learning backups.

Retention and deletion of local learning data

Learning data remains on the device until you reset it in Progress → Reset all learning data or delete the app. Resetting learning progress does not change the separate usage-sharing choice. Turn off sharing through Privacy & usage sharing if you also want to stop analytics. Delete exported backups separately from the places you saved them.

Families

The app works without usage sharing. Parents and guardians can keep sharing off and remove local learning data with the reset control or by deleting the app. The app requests general-audience ad content, but an App Store age rating is not an age-verification or consent mechanism. Please contact the developer with any privacy concerns involving a child's use of Bonjour Path.

Policy changes and contact

This policy will be updated when information practices change. The current published policy is available at Bonjour Path Privacy Policy.
