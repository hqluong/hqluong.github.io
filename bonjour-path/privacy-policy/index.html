Bonjour Path · Version 1.0

Privacy Policy

Bonjour Path is designed to keep learning personal. It requires no account and stores learning progress on your iPhone.

Effective September 4, 2026 Operator: Hung Luong

At a glance

Bonjour Path does not require an account, contain advertising, track you across apps or websites, or use a third-party analytics SDK. The app does not send personal information or learning activity to the app operator.

Information stored on your device

Bonjour Path stores the information needed to provide its learning features locally on your iPhone. This can include course progress, answers and attempts, review schedules, checkpoints, writing drafts, saved phrases, app preferences, reminder settings, and widget preferences.

This information remains in the app’s local storage unless you choose to export it.

Microphone and recordings

Microphone access is optional. Bonjour Path requests permission only when you choose to record a speaking-practice response. Recordings are created as temporary local files so you can replay or transcribe them. The app removes old temporary recordings and does not upload recordings to the app operator.

Speech recognition

Speech recognition is optional. When you ask what the device heard, Bonjour Path requires Apple’s on-device Canadian French speech recognition. If on-device recognition is unavailable, the app does not send the recording for remote transcription.

The resulting transcript is practice assistance—not an accent score, official assessment, or certification. Apple controls the operating-system permissions and speech-recognition technology; Apple’s handling of information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you enable learning reminders, Bonjour Path schedules local notifications through iOS. You can change notification permission and delivery in iPhone Settings. The app operator does not receive notification activity.

Encrypted learning backups

You may choose to export a password-protected encrypted backup of local learning data. The backup is encrypted on your device and is shared only through the destination you select. The app operator does not receive the backup or its password and cannot recover a forgotten password.

When you restore a compatible backup, Bonjour Path processes it locally on the device.

Collection, tracking, and sharing

Bonjour Path does not collect data from the app, track users across apps or websites, sell personal information, or share learning activity with advertisers or data brokers. A user-initiated export through the iOS share sheet is controlled by you and by the selected destination’s own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on your iPhone until you reset it in Bonjour Path or delete the app. The Progress screen includes a Reset all learning data control. Exported backups must be deleted separately from each location where you saved them.

Children’s privacy

Bonjour Path does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other user. A parent or guardian can remove locally stored app data by using the reset control or deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or information practices change. The current version and effective date will be posted on this page.
